## Product promise

LeLe Manager Pro is built around one maintained-knowledge promise:

> **LeLe Manager — Technical knowledge that stays accountable over time.**

The paid-product differentiator is not generic note-taking, local Markdown,
semantic search, AI chat, or opaque automation.

It is the ability to maintain approved technical knowledge so that it remains
reviewable, related, retrievable, explainable, and explicitly governed as it
evolves.

## Maintained-knowledge workflow

The maintained product contract is:

```text
Capture
  -> Validate
    -> Approve
      -> Relate
        -> Reuse
          -> Review
            -> Supersede
```

These stages are complementary responsibilities rather than automatic
authority escalation.

- **Capture** gathers raw knowledge through supported authoring and ingestion
  workflows.
- **Validate** checks structure, identity, references, and maintained Vault
  contracts.
- **Approve** keeps staged candidates separate from canonical knowledge until a
  human explicitly publishes them.
- **Relate** connects lessons through explicit typed relationships and canonical
  supersession metadata.
- **Reuse** retrieves maintained knowledge through explainable lexical,
  semantic, and metadata signals.
- **Review** surfaces freshness and potential contradiction evidence without
  declaring derived signals to be truth.
- **Supersede** records explicit knowledge evolution while preserving the
  history and identity of the knowledge being replaced.

The canonical Markdown Vault remains authoritative throughout this workflow.
Projection state, search indexes, similarity models, freshness signals, and
contradiction candidates remain derived or advisory according to their
maintained contracts.

## Pro readiness gates

The first Pro commercial-readiness milestone requires all four maintained
knowledge gates defined by epic #238:

- **#215 — Potential contradiction review**
  - surfaces explainable conflict candidates for human review;
  - does not silently rewrite, deprecate, or delete canonical knowledge.

- **#216 — Freshness and review-needed signals**
  - explains why knowledge may deserve review;
  - treats freshness as prioritization evidence, not a factual verdict.

- **#217 — Explicit typed relationships**
  - provides portable canonical relationships between LeLe entries;
  - preserves explicit human-controlled relationship and supersession
    semantics.

- **#219 — Explainable hybrid search**
  - combines lexical, semantic, and metadata evidence through one maintained
    retrieval boundary;
  - protects strong lexical evidence, supports bounded semantic assistance, and
    explains why results are relevant.

All four gates are implemented and integrated in the maintained product.

## Authority and explainability

Commercial readiness does not change LeLe Manager's authority model.

No heuristic, similarity model, ranking algorithm, freshness calculation, or
contradiction detector may silently mutate canonical knowledge.

Derived capabilities provide evidence and recommendations. Consequential
canonical changes still require the explicit application workflow and human
authority defined for that operation.

This is especially important for the Pro promise: maintained knowledge is not
knowledge that an AI automatically rewrites. It is knowledge whose evolution
remains inspectable and accountable.

## Local-first boundary

LeLe Manager remains local-first.

The Pro maintained-knowledge promise does not require:

- a hosted account;
- cloud knowledge storage;
- telemetry;
- hidden uploads;
- a remote AI provider;
- generic chatbot behavior.

Optional semantic capabilities complement deterministic local behavior and do
not become canonical authority.

## What this milestone means

Completion of epic #238 means the product can truthfully support the maintained
knowledge promise:

> **Technical knowledge that stays accountable over time.**

It means the minimum product capabilities needed for the first paid Pro offer
are integrated coherently.

It does **not** by itself define:

- Community versus Pro feature boundaries;
- pricing;
- licensing or activation mechanics;
- support tiers;
- sales or onboarding policy;
- release version or publication date.

Those commercial decisions remain separate follow-up work.

## Deferred capabilities

The first Pro offer does not depend on turning LeLe Manager into an AI-chat
product.

Features such as Context Packs, assistant-ready exports, Ask this Vault,
evidence-backed factual verification, richer provider integrations, and generic
chat behavior may strengthen later releases but are not required by this
readiness milestone.

The product remains centered on maintained technical knowledge rather than
chatbot breadth.
